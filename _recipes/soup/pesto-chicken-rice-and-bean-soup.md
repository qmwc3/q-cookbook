ingredients:
  - "2 pounds chicken thighs"
  - "2 tablespoons olive oil"
  - "1 yellow onion"
  - "2 carrots"
  - "4 cloves garlic"
  - "¼ cup sundried tomatoes"
  - "1 teaspoon salt"
  - "½ teaspoon pepper"
  - "1 teaspoons oregano"
  - "¼ teaspoons red pepper flakes"
  - "½ cup short-grain brown rice"
  - "4 cups chicken broth"
  - "30 ounces butter or pink beans"
  - "½ cup pesto"

preparations:
  - "Cook and chop the chicken into bite-size pieces"
  - "Chop the yellow onion."
  - "Chop the carrots."
  - "Finely chop the sundried tomatoes."
directions:
  - "In a large pot over medium heat, heat the oil."
  - "Add onion, carrots, garlic, tomatoes, salt, pepper, oregano, and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables soften (6&nbsp;minutes)."
  - "Add rice and stir until toasted (2&nbsp;minutes)."
  - "Add 2 cups broth and bring to a simmer, stirring occasionally."
  - "Cover and reduce heat to medium-low; let cook until rice is al dente (30&nbsp;minutes)."
  - "Return heat to medium and add beans and remaining broth. Stir to combine and continue cooking until slightly thickened and rice is completely tender (12&nbsp;minutes)."
  - "Remove from heat and stir in chicken and pesto."
